# Welcome to FacturaScan 360

Welcome to the **FacturaScan 360** business documentation. This site presents the full strategic foundation for the design, deployment, and scaling of a B2B SaaS product focused on automating invoice processing, semantic validation, and intelligent reporting for freelancers, SMEs, and accounting firms.

This documentation is divided into modular sections that reflect both the product development lifecycle and the business growth strategy.

Use the sidebar to explore the following areas:

- **Product Strategy and Validation**: Understand the problem we're solving, how the MVP is structured, and how we validated market fit through client feedback and pitch design.
- **Technical Architecture and Backend Cloud**: Explore the multi-tenant cloud setup, data isolation strategies, authentication via AWS Cognito, and relational data modeling.
- **Monetization and Financial Scaling**: Dive into the pricing models, billing automation via Stripe and AWS, and financial projections (MRR, ARR, CAC, LTV).
- **Design, Accessibility, and UX**: Learn how visual identity, accessibility standards (WCAG), and user flows contribute to a professional and inclusive product.
- **Data Science and Future Vision**: Discover how we structure data to enable future ML pipelines, analytics dashboards, and strategic decision-making.
- **Operations and Compliance**: Review legal and fiscal compliance for Spain (and future US expansion), documentation strategies, DevOps/MLOps principles, and technical support systems.
- **Marketing and Commercial Growth**: Examine our go-to-market strategy, competitive benchmarking, and channel development (partners, referrals, strategic alliances).

This project is built with a long-term vision: scalable, compliant, datified, and ready to evolve into a platform that blends automation with explainability.
